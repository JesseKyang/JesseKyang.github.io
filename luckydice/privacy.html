1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Lucky Dice, published by JesseKyang (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you use the app. Lucky Dice does not require you to create an account. Game data, including virtual Coin balances, Coins purchased or earned, and dice styles unlocked or purchased using Coins, is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud account, backup or synchronization service for this data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Lucky Dice may store app settings, preferences, game-related information, virtual Coin balances, rewarded-ad reward records and unlocked or purchased dice styles locally on your device. This includes Coins purchased through in-app purchases, Coins earned by watching rewarded advertisements, and all dice styles unlocked or purchased using those Coins.

All of this game-related information is stored locally on your device. We do not provide cloud backup, account-based synchronization or a data recovery service for these records. If you uninstall the app, clear its app data, reset your device or change to a new device, your Coin balance and unlocked or purchased dice styles may be permanently deleted and cannot be restored or recovered. This includes both unused purchased Coins and dice styles already unlocked using Coins.

We do not ordinarily receive this locally stored game content and cannot remotely retrieve or restore it after it has been lost.

3. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Lucky Dice uses Google AdMob's standard advertising functionality to provide banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded advertisements. We use standard AdMob features and settings and do not add our own advertising analytics or custom advertising APIs. The developer does not integrate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or other Firebase services, and does not operate a separate analytics or advertising server.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive certain information from the app or device, such as IP address, advertising identifiers or other device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and information used for ad delivery, reward validation, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement and personalization, depending on your device settings, consent choices, region and the services enabled.

When you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, the advertising provider may process information about the ad request and completion so the app can determine whether a reward is eligible. We do not control information collected directly by Google or its partners through their SDKs. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases, including Remove Ads and Coin packages, are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your full payment-card number or payment credentials. Google Play may process purchase details, transaction identifiers and related information to complete purchases, prevent fraud, provide purchase history and handle refunds. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. How Information Is Used

Locally stored data is used to operate the game, maintain your Coin balance, record rewarded-ad rewards, remember your unlocked or purchased dice styles and save your game preferences on that device. Information processed by third-party services may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, validate rewarded-ad completions, process purchases, prevent fraud, maintain security and diagnose technical issues.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate a user account database or upload your local Coin balance, dice collection or game settings to our own server. Google and other service providers may process information as needed to provide advertising, app distribution, billing, reward validation, security and diagnostics, subject to their own privacy practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game settings, Coin balances, rewarded-ad reward records and unlocked or purchased dice styles are stored locally on your device. This includes Coins purchased through Google Play, Coins earned through rewarded advertisements and dice styles unlocked or purchased using Coins.

If you uninstall Lucky Dice, clear the app's data, reset your device or change to a new device, these locally stored records may be permanently deleted. Any remaining purchased or earned Coins and all dice styles unlocked using Coins may be permanently lost and cannot be restored or recovered by us. We do not provide cloud backup, account-based synchronization or recovery services for this game data.

Data handled by Google AdMob or Google Play is retained and managed under Google's applicable policies and controls. The loss of locally stored game data does not necessarily delete Google Play purchase transaction records.

8. Children’s Privacy

Lucky Dice is not specifically directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account system. Advertising SDKs may process device information as described in their own policies. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided information to us may contact us.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps in designing and maintaining the app. However, no device, software or transmission method is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device and any backups.

10. International Use

Lucky Dice may be available worldwide. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be posted here with an updated date.
