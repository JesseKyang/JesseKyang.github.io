Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my Coins and unlocked dice colors stored?

Your Coin balance, Coins purchased or earned through rewarded advertisements, unused Coins, dice styles unlocked or purchased using Coins, and app settings are stored locally on your device.

Lucky Dice does not provide cloud backup, account-based synchronization or a data recovery service. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data, resetting or changing your device may permanently delete this information. Lost Coins and unlocked or purchased dice styles cannot be restored or recovered by us.

Google Play may retain records of your purchases, but those transaction records are separate from your locally stored in-app Coin balance and do not mean that lost Coins or dice styles can be restored.

How do I earn Coins?

Depending on availability, you may earn a small amount of Coins by choosing to watch and successfully completing a rewarded advertisement. Reward availability depends on ad supply, network conditions, eligibility and confirmation from the advertising provider.

Why can’t I watch rewarded ads after purchasing Remove Ads?

In Lucky Dice, Remove Ads disables all advertisements, including banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded advertisements. After purchasing Remove Ads, rewarded ads are no longer available and you cannot earn Coins by watching them.

I purchased a Coin package but did not receive the Coins.

First check that Google Play shows the purchase as completed. Restart the app and check your Coin balance again. If the Coins are still missing, email us with the Google Play order number, purchase date and app version.

Please do not send payment-card details. Also note that Google Play purchase records and your in-app Coin balance are separate. Since Coin balances are stored locally, a Google Play transaction record does not guarantee that lost or missing Coins can be recovered.

I purchased Remove Ads but still see ads.

Remove Ads disables all Lucky Dice advertisements, including banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded advertisements.

Check that you are using the same Google Play account used for the purchase and that the transaction is completed. Make sure the app is updated and restart it. If the issue continues, contact us with the Google Play order number and purchase date.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Review Google Play’s refund instructions to check eligibility and submit a request.

Why did I not receive a rewarded-ad Coin reward?

A reward is granted only when the rewarded advertisement is eligible and its completion is successfully confirmed. A network interruption, ad-provider issue, invalid completion or other technical problem may prevent a reward. If you believe there is an error, contact us with the approximate date and time.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us with the steps to reproduce the issue, your app version and device model. Screenshots can help; remove personal or sensitive information first.
