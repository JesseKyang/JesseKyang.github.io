Frequently Asked Questions

My wheels or spin history disappeared. Can you restore them?

Wheel data is stored locally on your device. If app data was cleared, the app was uninstalled, or the device was reset or replaced, the data may be permanently lost. Lucky Wheel does not provide cloud backup or account-based synchronization, and we cannot restore or recover lost wheel data.

I purchased Remove Ads, but I still see advertisements.

Remove Ads disables all advertisements in Lucky Wheel, including banner, interstitial and app-open advertisements.

If advertisements are still appearing, check that you are using the same Google Play account that made the purchase and that Google Play shows the purchase as completed. Make sure the app is updated and restart it. If the issue continues, email us with the purchase date and Google Play order details. Please do not send full payment or payment-card information.

I made a Support Coffee purchase. Where is my coffee or reward?

Support Coffee is an optional way to support the developer. It does not represent a physical coffee delivery and does not guarantee an additional in-app feature or reward. The product description shown in Google Play explains the purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Visit Google Play’s refund instructions to review eligibility and submit a request.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us with a clear description of the issue or suggestion, the steps to reproduce it, your app version and device model. Screenshots may help, but remove personal or sensitive information first.
