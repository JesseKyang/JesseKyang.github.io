1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Lucky Wheel, published by JesseKyang (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you use the app. Lucky Wheel does not require you to create an account. Wheel content, spin history, preferences and other related app records are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud account, backup or synchronization service for this data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter or generate in Lucky Wheel may be stored locally on your device. This may include wheel names, choices, colors, weights, spin results, history, preferences and app settings.

We do not ordinarily receive this locally stored content. It may be permanently deleted if you uninstall the app, clear its storage, reset your device or lose access to the device. We do not provide cloud backup, account-based synchronization or data recovery services for this information. You are responsible for maintaining your own device backups if needed.

3. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Lucky Wheel uses Google AdMob's standard advertising functionality to provide banner, interstitial and app-open advertisements. We use standard AdMob features and settings and do not add our own advertising analytics or custom advertising APIs. Lucky Wheel does not use rewarded advertisements. The developer does not integrate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or other Firebase services, and does not operate a separate analytics or advertising server.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive certain information from the app or device, such as IP address, advertising identifiers or other device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and information used for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement and personalization, depending on your device settings, consent choices, region and the services enabled.

We do not control information collected directly by Google or its partners through their SDKs. Please review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s information about advertising for details and available controls.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases, including Remove Ads and Support Coffee, are processed by Google Play. Remove Ads disables all advertisements displayed in Lucky Wheel. Support Coffee is an optional purchase that allows users to voluntarily support the developer; it does not represent the purchase of an actual coffee or guarantee additional app features.

We do not collect or store your full payment-card number or payment credentials. Google Play may process purchase details, transaction identifiers and related information to complete purchases, prevent fraud, provide purchase history and handle refunds. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Information

Locally stored wheel data is used by the app to provide its features, remember your wheel configurations and maintain spin history on your device. Information processed by third-party services may be used to provide and measure advertisements, process purchases, maintain security, prevent fraud and diagnose technical issues.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate a user account database or upload your locally stored wheel content, spin history or app settings to our own server. Google and other service providers may process information as necessary to provide advertising, app distribution, billing, security and diagnostics, subject to their own privacy practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Wheel content, spin history and app settings are stored locally on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. If you uninstall Lucky Wheel, clear its data or change devices, locally stored information may be permanently lost and cannot be restored or recovered by us.

We do not provide cloud backup, account-based synchronization or remote recovery services for local wheel data. We do not have access to retrieve or delete this local content remotely. Data handled by Google AdMob or Google Play is retained and managed under Google’s applicable policies and controls.

8. Children’s Privacy

Lucky Wheel is not specifically directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account system. Advertising SDKs may process device information as described in their own policies. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided information to us may contact us.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps in designing and maintaining the app. However, no device, software or transmission method is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device and any backups.

10. International Use

Lucky Wheel may be available worldwide. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be posted here with an updated date.
